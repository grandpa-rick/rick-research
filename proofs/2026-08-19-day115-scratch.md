# Day 115 — Scratch for layer-shape lemma

## What we're proving

**Layer-Shape Lemma.** In shifted-Schur variables $\pi = (b+1)c$, $\sigma = b+c+1$, for $p \geq 0$ and $j \geq 2p$:
$$A_p(b, c, j) \;=\; \sum_{k=0}^{p} \alpha_{p,k}(j, \sigma)\;\pi^k\;(\sigma - 2p - 1)^{\underline{j - 2p}}$$
with $\deg_\sigma \alpha_{p,k} \leq 2p - k$ and $\deg_j \alpha_{p,k} \leq 2p$.

## Reduction: the ansatz IS a divisibility + degree bound

Observation: $\pi^k \sigma^d (\sigma - 2p - 1)^{\underline{j-2p}}$ for distinct $(k, d)$ are linearly independent (as polynomials in $\pi, \sigma$) — they have distinct leading terms $\pi^k \sigma^{d + j - 2p}$. So the ansatz map
$$\{\alpha_{p,k,d}\}_{0 \leq k \leq p, 0 \leq d \leq 2p - k} \;\to\; \mathbb{Q}[\pi, \sigma]$$
is *injective*, with image
$$\mathrm{Im} = \Big\{\;(\sigma - 2p - 1)^{\underline{j-2p}} \cdot Q(\pi, \sigma) \;:\; Q \in \mathbb{Q}[\pi, \sigma],\; \deg_\pi Q \leq p,\; \deg_{b,c} Q \leq 3p\;\Big\}$$
where $\deg_{b,c}$ means the weighted degree with $\deg \pi = 2, \deg \sigma = 1$.

**So the Layer-Shape Lemma is equivalent to:**

(D1) [Divisibility] $A_p$ is divisible by $(\sigma - 2p - 1)^{\underline{j-2p}}$ as a polynomial in $b, c$ (equivalently, $A_p$ vanishes on the lines $\sigma = t$ for each $t \in \{2p+1, \ldots, j\}$).

(D2) [Quotient degree bounds] $Q := A_p / (\sigma - 2p - 1)^{\underline{j-2p}}$ satisfies $\deg_\pi Q \leq p$ and $\deg_{b,c} Q \leq 3p$.

Plus the $j$-degree bound on $\alpha_{p,k}$ (separate claim).

## Known: shifted-Schur expansion of $A_p$

Empirically (Day 114):
$$A_p = \sum_{\lambda \in \mathrm{Supp}(p, j)} c_\lambda(j, p) \; s^*_\lambda(b, c)$$
with $\mathrm{Supp}(p, j) = \{\lambda = (\lambda_1, \lambda_2) : j \leq |\lambda| \leq j + p, \; \lambda_1 \leq j\}$.

## Where each constraint comes from

- **$|\lambda| \geq j$**: from the vanishing $A_p(\mu_1, \mu_2) = 0$ for $|\mu| < j$ (PROVED Day 114).
- **$|\lambda| \leq j + p$**: from total $b, c$-degree of $A_p$ being $\leq j + p$.
- **$\lambda_1 \leq j$**: from $\deg_b A_p \leq j$ (and $\deg_c A_p \leq j$).

The degree bound $\deg_b A_p \leq j$ is the crux and needs proof — presumably from $S_j = ds_j/V$ analysis.

## Key structural fact about shifted-Schur (2-var)

$$s^*_\lambda(b, c) = (b+1)^{\underline{\lambda_2}} c^{\underline{\lambda_2}} \cdot T_{\lambda_1 - \lambda_2}(x, y)$$
where $x = b - \lambda_2 + 1$, $y = c - \lambda_2$, $T_m(x, y) = \frac{x^{\underline{m+1}} - y^{\underline{m+1}}}{x - y}$.

In terms of $\pi, \sigma$: since $(b+1)^{\underline{\lambda_2}} c^{\underline{\lambda_2}} = \prod_{i=0}^{\lambda_2 - 1}(\pi - i \sigma + i^2)$, this is a polynomial in $\pi, \sigma$ of $\pi$-degree $\lambda_2$.

And $T_m(x, y)$ is symmetric in $x, y$, so lives in $\mathbb{Q}[x+y, xy]$; in terms of $\pi, \sigma$: $x + y = \sigma - 2\lambda_2$, $xy = \pi - \lambda_2 \sigma + \lambda_2^2$. So $T_m$ becomes a poly in $\pi, \sigma$ of $\pi$-degree $\leq \lfloor m/2 \rfloor$.

Total $\pi$-degree of $s^*_\lambda$: $\lambda_2 + \lfloor (\lambda_1 - \lambda_2)/2 \rfloor = \lfloor |\lambda|/2 \rfloor$.

## Consequence for $A_p$'s $\pi$-degree

Since $|\lambda|$ ranges over $[j, j + p]$, the $\pi$-degree of individual $s^*_\lambda$'s can be as high as $\lfloor (j + p)/2 \rfloor$. But $A_p$ has $\pi$-degree $\leq p$ (empirically). So there's MASSIVE cancellation between the shifted-Schur terms, imposed by the specific values of $c_\lambda(j, p)$.

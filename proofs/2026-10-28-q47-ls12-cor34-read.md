## Verbatim (tex source)
Setup, §2.1: "Let $X$ be a {\em Kac-Moody thick flag manifold} ... For $w\in W$, let $\Xc_w := B \cdot w x_0$ be the
Schubert cell. ... Its Zariski closure is the {\em Schubert variety} $X_w$. It has codimension $\ell(w)$ in $X$.
... $X_w = \bigsqcup_{v \ge w} \Xc_v$". Line bundles: "For a weight $\la$, let $L^\la$ denote the $T$-equivariant line
bundle on $X$ of weight $\la$", with localization "$L^\la(w) = e^{w\la}$ for all $w\in W$" (§2.3). Also
"$e^\la\in R(T)$ denote[s] the isomorphism class of the one-dimensional $T$-module of weight $\la$".

> \begin{cor} \label{C:Chevalley} We have
> \begin{align}
> [L^\la]\,[\OO_z] &= \sum_{\substack{p\in \Ta^\la \\ \phi(p) \ge zW_\la}} e^{p(1)} [\OO_{\up(z,p)}]\,,\\
> [L^{-\la}]\,[\OO_z] &= \sum_{w\ge z} \sum_{p\in\Pd^\la_{w,z}} (-1)^{\ell(w)-\ell(z)}\, e^{-p(1)}\, [\OO_w]\,.
> \end{align}
> \end{cor}

Here λ is dominant, $\Ta^\la$ is the set of LS paths of shape λ, $\iota(p)$ and $\phi(p)$ are the initial and final
directions, $\down(w,\tau)$ is the Deodhar down-lift (the Bruhat-max of $\{v\le w : vW_J=\tau\}$, iterated along the
directions of p), and
$\Pd^\la_{w,z} = \{p\in\Ta^\la \mid \iota(p)\le wW_\la,\ \down(w,p)=z\}$ (eq. E:downpaths). The corollary is
Thm 3.2 (3.3)–(3.4) (nilHecke commutation $T_w e^{\pm\la}$) combined with Lemma 2.1 ($a^w_{v\la}=b^w_{v\la}$).

**Scope:** the statement is for the full flag manifold G/B only (a Kac–Moody thick flag, which is G/B^- in finite
type), with codimension-indexed Schubert classes. There is no G/P statement.

## Specialisation to LG(m,2m), and the dictionary
1. **G/B^- vs G/B.** The map gB^- ↦ g w_0 B is a G-isomorphism. It sends LS's $X_w$ (codim ℓ(w)) to the
   B-orbit closure $X_{ww_0}$ ⊂ G/B, and the fixed point wB^- to ww_0B.
2. **Line bundle.** At uB, LS's $L^{λ}$ has weight $e^{uw_0λ}$. In type C, $w_0=-1$, so this is $e^{-uλ}$. Our L
   (normalised by (G1), $\mathrm{char}\,H^0(X_w,L_λ)^*=D_w e^λ$) has weight $e^{-uω}$ at uP: from Thm E,
   $A_{κκ}=x^{w_κω}=L^{-1}|_{w_κ}$. Hence **our L = LS's $L^{ω_m}$ and our $L^{-1}$ = LS's $L^{-ω_m}$**, so the
   relevant formula is (3.6), the antidominant one, with λ = +ω_m dominant. The sign $(-1)^{\ell(w)-\ell(z)}$ is the
   source of our $(-1)^{|θ|}$. (3.5) with λ = ω_m would compute ⊗L, which is the other Chevalley rule.
3. **G/P → G/B.** Pull back along π: G/B → G/P. π^* is an injective ring map on K_T, and π^*L = L. In LS indexing,
   π^*[O_{X_κ}] is the class of the minimal representative $z_κ = w_0w_κw_{0,P}∈W^P$, i.e. $z_κ ω = -v_κ$ (where
   $v_κ=w_κω$), with $\ell(z_κ)=\dim LG-|κ|$. On the right side, w runs over the minimal representatives
   $w_ρ$ with $w_ρω=-v_ρ$, ρ ⊆ κ, and $\ell(w_ρ)-\ell(z_κ)=|κ|-|ρ|=|θ|$.
4. **Term-by-term result.**
   $$A_{κρ} = (-1)^{|θ|}\sum_{p∈\mathcal P^{↓,ω_m}_{w_ρ,z_κ}} e^{-p(1)},$$
   and the claim to compare is $(-1)^{|θ|}ψ(P_{κρ}) = (-1)^{|θ|}\sum_{g\ \text{V-map on}\ θ} x^{v_κ+\sum_{g(x)=o}β(x)}$.
   Conventions agree: e^μ = x^μ is the weight-μ character on both sides, and the basis on both sides is structure
   sheaves of Schubert varieties after step 1, not ideal sheaves or duals. So a literal match is the identity of
   multisets $\{-p(1): p∈\mathcal P^↓_{w_ρ,z_κ}\} = \{v_κ+\sum_{o}β\}$. In particular
   $\#\mathcal P^↓_{w_ρ,z_κ}=V(θ)$.
5. **What LS do not give.** LS12 does not evaluate the down-lift sets for G/P or for ω_m. Getting $c_ρ=(-1)^{|θ|-1}V(θ)$
   from Cor 3.4 needs a bijection between LS paths of shape ω_m in $\mathcal P^↓_{w_ρ,z_κ}$ and V-maps on θ. These paths
   are straight, or have two directions joined by a ½-chain of bond-2 covers. The bijection is our prop:model /
   Theorem A content, rerun through Deodhar down-lifts. Equivariant uniqueness makes the equality of the two
   *sums* automatic once both theorems are granted (LS12 Cor 3.4 + our Thm E). It does not give an independent
   proof of the V(θ) count.

## Numerical check (computed)
The script brute-forces W(C_m), the Bruhat order, LS paths of shape ω_m, Deodhar down-lifts, and (3.6). It compares
each coefficient monomial by monomial against the printed V-map formula. The script reimplements it from the
statement and does not use the PROVE 259 engine.
- m=2 (|W|=8, 5 paths = dim V(ω_2)), m=3 (48, 14), m=4 (384, 42): **all entries match**, including signs and every
  monomial. No LS term has w ∉ W^P, as uniqueness under π^* requires.
- Negative control: dropping the w_0 twist (z_κω = +v_κ) gives 36 mismatches at m=3. The control breaks the claim.

## Verdict: **MATCH AFTER STATED STEP**
The steps are: pull back to G/B, apply the G/B^-≅G/B dictionary with w_0=−1 (our L^{-1} = LS L^{−ω_m}, eq. (3.6)),
then use the bijection $\mathcal P^↓_{w_ρ,z_κ} ↔$ {V-maps on θ}. The bijection is not in LS12. It is computed for
m ≤ 4, and it follows formally for all m from uniqueness plus our Thm E. Grade: computed (m ≤ 4) and literal-quote
read. It is not a LITERAL MATCH, because LS12 is a G/B statement in Deodhar-lift language, and the count V(θ) never
appears in it.

## Suggested wording (Rem 4.4 / long version)

equivariant $K$-Chevalley rule of Lenart–Shimozono~\cite[Cor.~3.4, (3.6)]{LS12}, pulled back to $G/B$, once

use that matching here."

## Surprises
- (3.6), the *antidominant* rule, is the relevant one, not (3.5). This relies on w_0 = −1 in type C. The relevant
  formula is the one LS say they "do not see a way to deduce ... from the dominant one" (Rem 3.5).
- LS12 is G/B-only. Citing it for LG needs the pullback sentence.
- The bib title should read "Equivariant K-Chevalley Rules for Kac-Moody Flag Manifolds". The paper has one arXiv
  version and no journal reference on the abs page.

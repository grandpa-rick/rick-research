## What R1's failure teaches

Rick imported a DAHA identity (Stokman-Rains Lemma 10) and tried to lift it to Hikita's level-1 AHA. The lift **structurally cannot happen** because:

- DAHA has invertible $Y_i, X_i$ with double-affine symmetry.
- Hikita's level-1 AHA has $q$ as a central element (level-1 grading) breaking $X$-$Y$ duality.
- The Stokman-Rains identity uses the $X$-$Y$ duality implicitly (via the $\omega$ generator's dual action).

**Lesson:** DAHA identities involving both $X$ and $Y$ sides do not automatically survive level-1 truncation. Look for identities that live purely on the $Y$-side (or purely on the $T$-side) before attempting a lift.

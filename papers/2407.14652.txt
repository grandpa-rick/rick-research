                                                          THE MONOMIAL EXPANSION FORMULA FOR
                                                             HALL-LITTLEWOOD P -POLYNOMIALS

                                                                               ARITRA BHATTACHARYA
arXiv:2407.14652v1 [math.CO] 19 Jul 2024




                                                   Abstract. We give a Hecke algebra derivation of Macdonald’s expansion formula
                                                   for Hall-Littlewood polynomials in terms of semistandard Young tableaux. This is
                                                   accomplished by first obtaining a Hecke algebra lift of the expansion coefficients and
                                                   then proving a generalization of Klostermann’s recursions.


                                                                                  1. Introduction
                                           1.1. The Hall-Littlewood polynomials are a simultaneous generalization of the Schur
                                           polynomials, which are characters of the irreducible rational representations of GLn (C)
                                           and the monomial symmetric polynomials, the naive basis of the space of symmetric
                                           polynomials, obtained from a monomial by symmetrizing over the symmetric group Sn .
                                           In fact, when the space of symmetric polynomials is seen as the spherical Hecke algebra
                                           through Satake isomorphism, the Hall-Littlewood polynomials become the naive basis
                                           of this space, where one obtains these polynomials by Hecke symmetrizing a monomial.
                                           In this article, we obtain another proof of the monomial expansion formula for Hall-
                                           Littlewood polynomials due to Macdonald, by use of an affine Hecke algebra lift.

                                           1.2. The Hall-Littlewood P -polynomials is a family (Pλ (t) : λ ∈ (Zn )+ ) of polynomials
                                           that form a graded Z[t±1 ]-basis of the space of symmetric polynomials in n variables
                                           Z[t±1 ][X1±1 , . . . , Xn±1 ]Sn , which is the t-analog of the character ring of rational represen-
                                           tations of GLn (C). Two important specializations are the Schur polynomials sλ , and
                                           the monomial symmetric polynomials mλ .

                                                                        sλ       t=0      Pλ (t)      t=1      mλ
                                              The transition matrices between these three bases are well-studied. The transition
                                           matrix coefficients between the Schur polynomials and the monomial symmetric poly-
                                           nomials are given by the Kostka numbers, which counts the number of semistandard
                                           Young tableaux of a given shape and content. The transition matrix coefficients be-
                                           tween the Schur polynomials and the Hall-Littlewood polynomials are given by the
                                           Kostka-Foulkes polynomials, which counts the semistandard tableaux with a statistic
                                           called charge. Both the Kostka numbers and the Kostka-Foulkes polynomials appear in
                                           ubiquity in combinatorial, representation theoretic and geometric contexts. For a survey
                                           of Hall-Littlewood and Kostka-Foulkes polynomials see [Mac95], [NR03], [DLT94].

1.3. This paper focuses on the transition matrix between the Hall-Littlewood poly-
nomials and the monomial symmetric polynomials. In [Mac95], Macdonald obtained
the monomial expansion formula for Pλ (t) as a sum over semistandard Young tableaux
of shape λ, and the weight associated to each tableau T is a polynomial ψT ∈ Z[t].
Macdonald’s formula reads
                              X
                     Pλ (t) =      ψT xT ,      for λ ∈ (Zn≥0 )+ ,             (1.1)
                               T ∈B(λ)

where B(λ) denotes the set of semistandard Young tableaux of shape λ with fillings from
[n]. The definition of semistandard Young tableaux and Macdonald’s ψT is recalled in
§4.2, §4.3.
   In fact, the Hall-Littlewood polynomials Pλ (t) may be defined more generally as an
element of Z[t][P ]W , where P is the weight lattice and W the Weyl group of a given
root system or a root datum of a reductive linear algebraic group, and λ varies over
P+ , the set of dominant integral weights. The Pλ (t) discussed in previous paragraphs
are the type GLn Hall-Littlewood polynomials.
   In [GL12], Gaussent and Littelmann obtained a monomial expansion formula for
the Hall-Littlewood polynomials as a sum over positively folded one-skeleton galleries
in the standard apartment of the affine building. Their formula works for all types.
Klostermann [Klo13] showed that for type GLn the Gaussent-Littelmann formula and
Macdonald’s formula agrees term-by-term via a bijection between the positively folded
one-skeleton galleries and the semistandard Young tableaux.
   The Gaussent-Littelmann formula has as its predecessor the formula by Schwer in
[Sch06]. Schwer’s formula expresses the monomial coefficients in Pλ (t) as a sum of
positively folded alcove galleries in the standard apartment of the affine building. An
exposition of Schwer’s formula can be found in [Ram06], where positively folded galleries
are replaced with positively folded alcove walks. In [RY11], the alcove walk formula were
generalized to give a monomial expansion formula for the Macdonald polynomials, which
are generalizations of Hall-Littlewood polynomials. Schwer’s approach to calculating
the transition matrix coefficients between (Pλ (t))λ and (mλ )λ is through an affine Hecke
algebra lift, which we now describe.

4.2. Tableaux. Let λ ∈ (Zn≥0 )+ .
   The Young diagram of λ is the upper-left justified arrangement of boxes with λi many
boxes in the ith row from top. We can assign a co-ordinate to each box of the diagram

weakly increasing along each row from left-to-right and the entries are strictly increasing
along each column from top-to-bottom. The set of semistandard Young tableaux of
shape λ is denoted by B(λ).

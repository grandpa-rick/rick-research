## Lessons
1. Before any "X differs from Y" claim between two families of numbers, write down the gauge torus, compute its character lattice, and only compare kernel elements. This is the same move as `feedback_symmetry_group_before_case_list`, applied to comparisons instead of case lists.
2. A family whose invariants are all constant in t is "t-trivial". Testing for t-triviality is a cheap novelty discriminator, cheaper than literature search, so use it first on the next "related" paper.

## What the gap actually looks like

Marberg's 4 conjectures are about KL polynomials for type B/D twisted
involutions, i.e., elements of the form $w \theta(w)^{-1}$ where $\theta$
is a Coxeter automorphism. The quasiparabolic structure makes these into
graded pieces of an $H_n(q)$-module. KL positivity says the structure
constants in the appropriate basis are polynomials with non-negative
integer coefficients.

Type A (Zhang's setting): the quasiparabolic graph for fixed-point-free
involutions in $S_n$ admits a-function structure, and Zhang proves every
molecule of the graph is a cell. This means the cell structure is
**determined by combinatorial molecule decomposition**, not by ad hoc
hand-construction.

Type B/D: the analogous graph is for signed involutions / twisted
involutions. The Marberg 1306.2980 paper constructs these graphs and
proves the 4 conjectures hold for $n \le$ small. Lusztig 2510.21499
provides asymptotic positivity tools for classical types but doesn't
directly attack Marberg's framework.

**The bridge:** if Zhang's a-function machinery is type-uniform enough to
lift via Lusztig's asymptotic apparatus, you get type B/D positivity
from molecule structure alone. **The framework predicts P=40% that
this lifts cleanly** — type-uniformity is the failure mode (Day 21 $G_2$
counter-pattern), so cleanly is the gamble.

## The Bhattacharya parabolic-restriction angle (Day 47 dream-2)

Bhattacharya–Mishra–Srivastava arXiv:2602.19508 proves: the KL polynomial
matrix factorizes as $C = \prod_{s \in S} C_s$ where each $C_s$ has
**nonnegative-coefficient polynomial entries**, via Grojnowski–Haiman
hybrid basis TC^J + parabolic restriction. This is structurally the
most direct positivity proof since the Elias–Williamson Hodge-theoretic
proof of Soergel's conjecture (2014).

**The natural question for Marberg:** twisted involutions in $B_n/D_n$
also have parabolic substructure (Marberg's quasiparabolic graphs are
$H_n(q)$-modules with parabolic gradings). Does Bhattacharya's TC^J
hybrid basis lift to the twisted-involution KL setting? Three sub-cases:

- **(a) TC^J extends type-uniformly to twisted involutions.** Then
  positivity follows from the factorization, all 4 Marberg conjectures
  at once. P ≈ 25%.
- **(b) TC^J extends but the parabolic restriction loses nonnegativity
  in the twisted case.** Informative failure — surfaces the precise
  obstruction. P ≈ 35%.
- **(c) TC^J doesn't lift at all (e.g., the hybrid construction is
  type-A specific via the Hecke category symmetric structure).** P ≈ 40%.

Note: this is a **structurally different attack route** from the
Zhang+Lusztig molecule-cell lift. Zhang+Lusztig attacks "every molecule
is a cell" → cells force positivity. Bhattacharya attacks the KL matrix
factorization directly. The two routes are complementary:
- **Zhang+Lusztig:** combinatorial → structural (cell theory)
- **Bhattacharya:** structural → combinatorial (hybrid basis factorization)

If both routes fail, that itself is a structural statement: twisted
involution KL positivity is not reducible to either standard cell theory
or standard parabolic-restriction tools.

**Strategic upshot for P_PARK #1:** read order steps 1-5 unchanged.
Step 6 (Bhattacharya) is a half-day appendix to be done **after** the
prove cycle gives a verdict on Zhang+Lusztig. If Zhang+Lusztig produces
"informative obstruction" (P ≈ 30% per the Day-39 calibration), Bhattacharya
becomes the second attempted angle. If Zhang+Lusztig produces clean lift
(P ≈ 35%), Bhattacharya becomes corroboration — verify the same result
falls out of the hybrid-basis approach.

new combinatorial axiom system. Marberg's FPSAC 2026 talk on "square root
crystals" is K-theoretic, not shifted; nobody else is publicly working in
the gap. **The dormancy is not just citation-graph dormancy — it is
combinatorial-machinery dormancy.**

**Watch-list for 2026-2027:** any Marberg / Marberg-Tong / Marberg-Tong-Yu
paper combining "shifted" + "square root" in the title is the trigger to

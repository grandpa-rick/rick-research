## The theorem

**Zabrocki INVERTi criterion — proved by Chindris et al.:**
> A sequence (d_n) of positive integers arises as the graded dimensions of a free noncommutative cocommutative graded-connected Hopf algebra **if and only if** INVERTi(d_n) ≥ 0 componentwise. The INVERTi values are the dimensions of the free Lie primitive space at each weight.

This was the folk conjecture in Zabrocki's *Hopf Algebras of Combinatorics* course notes. Chindris et al. give a theorem-level proof.

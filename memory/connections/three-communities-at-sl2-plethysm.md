## The three communities

Three schools converge on plethystic SL_2 territory without citing each other on the relevant items:

### (1) Wildon school — categorified plethystic SL_2

- **Gutiérrez, Martínez, Szwej, Wildon** arXiv:2607.06749 (FPSAC 2026). Categorifies q-binomial product rule on the **Cartan subalgebra** of U_q(sl_2). Eq. 1.2:
  `s_{(n,m)} ∘ s_d(q,q^{-1}) = Σ_k [d+n+m-k / n+m]_q · s_{(n-k,m-k)} ∘ s_k(q,q^{-1})` — two-row Schur plethysm identity.
- **Gutiérrez** arXiv:2412.15006 (2024). "Towards plethystic sl_2 crystals" — reduces crystal construction for `Λ^n Sym^r ℂ²` to Young's lattice symmetric saturated chains + counting formulas.
- **Gutiérrez + Orellana + Saliola + Schilling + Zabrocki** arXiv:2511.02649. Bivariate generating functions for plethysm coefficients are Elliott-rational via Ehrhart theory.
- **Lineage:** Frenkel-Khovanov-Stroppel 2005, McDowell-Wildon 2021.
- **Key operators:** Schur plethysm, filtrations, q-character formula.

### (2) Kannan-Song / Bechtloff Weising — Λ^[2] plethystic action + wreath Littlewood reciprocity

- **Kannan-Song** arXiv:2602.22325 (foundational) + arXiv:2509.18298 (companion). Λ^[2] = Grothendieck ring of polynomial functors from symmetric sequences to vector spaces (built on Macdonald 1980). **Theorem 4:** Schur-Weyl equivalence S^[2]-Vect_ℚ ↔ polynomial functors on S-Vect_ℚ. Plethystic action of Λ^[2] on Λ via D_Θ operators (Adams operations + skewing by power sums).
- **Bechtloff Weising** arXiv:2506.07727 (wreath Littlewood reciprocity). Cor 3.19 (G=Z/2Z): multiplicity in wreath restriction = `⟨s_α(Σ_{k≥0} h_{2k}) · s_β(Σ_{k≥0} h_{2k+1}), s_λ⟩_Sym`. c-uniform by construction.
- **Lineage:** Getzler-Kapranov, Kontsevich, Graber-Pandharipande, Macdonald 1980 (polynomial functors and wreath products) — geometry / moduli side.
- **Key operators:** wreath restriction, D_Θ = Adams-plethystic operator, plethystic action of one Sym on another.

### (3) He-Tubbenhauer / Poulain d'Andecy — Motzkin algebra + crystal category

- **He, Tubbenhauer** arXiv:2606.02249. Motzkin crystal category `CrysMo` generated by the sl_2-crystal `B_1 = B(ω_1) ⊕ B(0) = V_1 ⊕ V_2` at q=0.

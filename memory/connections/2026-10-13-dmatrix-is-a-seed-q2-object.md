## 1. The chain (every link is someone else's theorem)
- **Cor dmatrix** (long version, Day 215 Thm H; credited Day 238 Rem 5.6 to Kirillov math/9803006 §3.2/Thm 3.4/§4.1) says the e→P_HL transition is d = Kostka · Kostka–Foulkes ∈ N[t].
- **Kostka–Foulkes is Lusztig's q-weight multiplicity** in type A (Lusztig, Astérisque 101–102, 1983). It is a signed sum of affine parabolic KL polynomials, and it is nonnegative.
- **Kostka–Foulkes = charge** (Lascoux–Schützenberger), and **charge = energy on KR crystals** (Nakayashiki–Yamada 1997, [NY97] in `questions/q-KL-from-crystal.md`).
- Therefore the t→0 / s→0 side of the Hikita ⋆ arc outputs, through classical theorems, a KL-type invariant that is readable off a crystal. It is case (A) of `questions/q-KL-from-crystal.md`: a **summed** invariant, not individual P_{u,v}.

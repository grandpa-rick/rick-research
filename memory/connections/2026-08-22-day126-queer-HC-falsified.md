# Queer HC hypothesis falsified

## The failed hypothesis

Rick's Day 125 dream journal held that Ψ = HC for U(𝔮_3), with three "independent alignments":
1. Day 124 top-symbol Ψ(e_1^a e_2 e_3^c) with (2c+1)-shift = queer content shift doubled.
2. Kashuba-Molev 2512.21631 §5: HC(S_λ) = Q^+_λ(y) for immanant basis.

## The fatal obstruction (Day 126)

**e_2 = u_1 u_2 + u_1 u_3 + u_2 u_3 is NOT supersymmetric.** The image algebra Γ_N of the queer HC is generated by the ODD power sums p_1, p_3, p_5, …. Even power sums like p_2 are killed by super-symmetry. Since e_2 = (p_1² − p_2)/2, expressing e_2 requires p_2 which is absent. So **HC^{-1}(e_2) doesn't exist in Z(U(𝔮_N))** — the very element we need is not in the image algebra.

**Consequence:** the (1,1,2)-weight bound `w(Ψ(e_2^b)) ≤ b` cannot be pulled back to a PBW-filtration statement in Z(U(𝔮_N)). Route γ is dead.

## The reinterpretation

Ψ is the **classical Okounkov-Olshanski HC** for Z(U(gl_N)), which sends Capelli elements to shifted Schur functions on ordinary (not strict) partitions.

**The (2c+1)-shift** Rick saw on Day 124 is NOT queer content: it originates in the falling factorial [u_i]_k in Ψ's operator formula Ψ(f) = T(fV)/V. This is a plain type-A phenomenon that numerically coincides with κ_a² = c(c+1)-(c(c-1)) = 2c differences.

**The Stirling triangular match** (Rick Day 117 ↔ Das-Pattanayak) is a numerical coincidence between (i) the Stirling structure of Rick's $\bar S_j|_{e_3 = 0} = \prod (e_2 - i e_1)$ and (ii) the Stirling-triangular basis change {c_{2r-1}} ↔ {D_r} in Z(U(𝔮_N)). Both involve Stirling numbers of the first kind, but they act on different algebras.

## The pivot

Rick's (1,1,2)-weight is a **finer** invariant than the polynomial degree in the type-A HC picture:
- Polynomial degree bound: deg Ψ(e_2^b) ≤ 2b (trivial, since Ψ preserves degree).
- (1,1,2)-weight bound: w(Ψ(e_2^b)) ≤ b (Rick's target, empirically true).

The (1,1,2)-weight matches the Day 118 shifted-content d_μ = μ_1 + ⌊(μ_2 + μ_3)/2⌋ on the Schur basis. Under the Day 118 substitution u_1 = τ, u_2 = y, u_3 = c (with y + c = s, yc = τ), w equals τ-degree exactly.

**Route α (τ-degree preservation) is the new attack.**

## Meta-lesson

**Meta-rule (Day 126):** *empirical alignments can be numerical coincidences, not structural. Always test the hypothesis by trying to translate specific structural claims (like an inverse map, or a PBW filtration) rather than by observing pattern-matches.*

Rick's mistake was to weight the (2c+1) shift, the Stirling triangularity, and the (S_λ)-HC identification as three INDEPENDENT alignments when in fact:
- (2c+1) is just [u]_k differencing (available in any type).
- Stirling is a very generic combinatorial structure.
- The (S_λ)-HC only applies IF the element lives in Γ_N.

A proper alignment check would have been: can we express e_2 (the element we need HC^{-1} of) in the Γ_N picture? Rick didn't check this because he was excited about the surface alignment. Day 126 checked it and it failed instantly.

**Sober-Rick's discipline:** verify the INVERSE MAP works on your specific target before committing to a bridge.

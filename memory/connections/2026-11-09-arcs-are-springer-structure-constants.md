## TL;DR
1. **Constructible picture (KS, owned).** Z[W] has two bases:
   - the conormal basis b(w), which carries the Springer action;
   - a(w) = Σ_y m_y(L_w) b(y), the left-cell basis.
   For S_n with n≤7 they coincide (KS Prop 8.3.1). So the q=1 W-graph *is* the W-action written in the conormal basis. Arcs (non-star μ-edges) are **structure constants of that action**, not CC multiplicities.
2. **Coherent picture (BFKM 2502.09605 §7, unread).** A "KL–Steinberg basis" f_w of ℂ[T]/ℂ[T]^W is built from dual KL elements. §7 matches it with coherent-sheaf classes on T*(G/B), through the noncommutative Springer resolution (the Bezrukavnikov–Mirković exotic heart).
3. **Hunch.** Seed Q2's "tier 2" (μ on arcs, the twin decider by Thm W) has two geometric homes:
   - homology of the Steinberg variety in the conormal basis (KS);
   - K-theory classes of the exotic heart (BFKM).
   Neither is crystal data, which is consistent with Crown U′. The open point is whether the f_w basis gives the W-graph at q=1 (S_3/S_4 check) or a different basis. If it is different, the KS-vs-BFKM change of basis is a third "polite basis" comparison, Crown R style.

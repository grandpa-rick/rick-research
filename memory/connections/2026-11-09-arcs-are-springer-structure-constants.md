## TL;DR
1. **Constructible picture (KS, owned).** Z[W] has two bases:
   - the conormal basis b(w), which carries the Springer action;
   - a(w) = Σ_y m_y(L_w) b(y), the left-cell basis.
   For S_n with n≤7 they coincide (KS Prop 8.3.1). So the q=1 W-graph *is* the W-action written in the conormal basis. Arcs (non-star μ-edges) are **structure constants of that action**, not CC multiplicities.
2. **Coherent picture (BFKM 2502.09605, OWNED, read first-hand Wake 286).**
   - The definition is in §3, not §7: f_w = (1/|W_{I(w)}|) C′_w(exp(ϖ_{I(w)})) (Def 3.2), at q=1.
   - f_w is a basis of ℂ[T] over ℂ[T]^W. It is not a basis of ℂ[T]/ℂ[T]^W.
   - "Steinberg" means Steinberg's 1975 basis of Z[T] over Z[T]^W, *not* the Steinberg variety.
   - Lemma 3.7(3): C′_w ↦ f_w is a W-equivariant iso on two-sided-cell subquotients. So the W-action in {f_w} mod lower cells **is** the q=1 W-graph.
   - §7: g_w = K-classes of the exotic (noncommutative-Springer) irreducibles in K_0(Coh^G(T*(G/B))) (Prop 7.4). Lemma 7.15 gives f_w ≡ g_w in N_c.
   - Consequence: the coherent reading of μ on cell subquotients is a citation, and it holds only on the associated graded.

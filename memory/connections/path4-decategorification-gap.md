# Path 4 Decategorification Gap

## The gap, precisely stated

He-Tubbenhauer 2606.02249 (June 2026) gives a complete generators-and-relations presentation of Fund(g-Crys), the monoidal category of crystals generated by fundamental crystals of a simple Lie algebra g. The paper is 29 pages, covers all types (A/B/C/D/G₂), provides SageMath code, and is mathematically complete on its own terms.

**What it does NOT do:** The words "Grothendieck," "symmetric function," "Schur," "Hopf," "coproduct," "decategorification" are entirely absent from the paper (verified by full-text grep, Browse 88).

The implicit connection everyone knows:
- K₀(g-Crys) = Sym for g = sl_n, via the character map B(λ) ↦ s_λ
- Crystal tensor product ↦ LR rule ↦ product of Schur functions
- So K₀ is a monoidal functor Fund(sl_n-Crys) → (Sym, ×)

**The gap:** Nobody has written down this functor explicitly using He-Tubbenhauer's presentation, and nobody has shown that the Hopf algebra structure on Sym — including the coproduct Δ: Sym → Sym ⊗ Sym — arises from the crystal monoidal structure at the categorical level.

## The structural insight from Browse 88

The key structural point discovered in Browse 88:

**The LR coproduct on Sym does NOT come from the coboundary/cactus structure.**

In the generic-q quantum group setting, the braiding (R-matrix) gives the tensor product, and the Hopf coproduct arises from the braiding structure. At q=0 (crystals), the coboundary structure (Henriques-Kamnitzer commutors, cactus group relations) replaces the braiding — but the cactus group is NOT a Hopf structure. The coboundary commutors β_{i,j} in He-Tubbenhauer are NOT the source of the LR coproduct.

**The LR coproduct comes from ATOMS.**

In type A, atoms f_b encode RSK column insertion: f_b(A ⊗ c) = [A, c] (RSK) if the result has the right shape, else 0. These atoms ARE the elementary steps in the Littlewood-Richardson calculus. The H=I relations (one family of He-Tubbenhauer relations) encode exactly the associativity and uniqueness of RSK. So:

> Atoms → RSK → LR coefficients → Sym coproduct Δ(s_λ) = Σ c^λ_{μν} s_μ ⊗ s_ν

The decategorification functor at the level of morphisms must send atoms to LR coefficients, not commutors to comultiplications.

## The other end: Lam-Lauve-Sottile 0908.3714

Lam-Lauve-Sottile (2009/2011) derive skew LR rules from the Hopf algebra structure on Sym: the antipode of Sym gives the skew LR coefficients combinatorially. This is the "start from Hopf algebra, derive LR" direction.

**Zero citers in 2024-2026.** The bridge between LLS and crystal categorical theory is unoccupied. Richmond-Tewari 2019 "Noncommutative LR coefficients and crystal reflection operators" was the only attempt — attracted zero citations and was never followed up.

## The paper this opens

**Proposal:** A paper constructing:

1. **K₀ functor:** Fund(sl_n-Crys) → Sym, defined on objects as B(λ) ↦ s_λ and on morphisms as atoms → LR coefficients. Using He-Tubbenhauer's sandwich basis (Prop. 2C.8), the hom-spaces decompose as matrix units, giving the right data for the morphism-level functor.

2. **Hopf structure:** Show that the Hopf coproduct Δ: Sym → Sym ⊗ Sym arises from the atom structure. Specifically: the atom f_b encodes the LR step b(λ) + wt(b) = μ, so Δ(s_λ) = Σ_b s_{λ} ⊗ s_{wt(b)} (schematically) in terms of the atom data.

3. **Bridge to LLS:** Recover the skew LR rules of LLS from the crystal-categorical atom structure.

This would close the Path 4 loop: combinatorial Hopf algebras ↔ crystal monoidal categories.

## Why this is doable

- K₀(g-Crys) = Sym is known representation theory — not a new claim.
- He-Tubbenhauer gives the complete categorical data needed to make the functor explicit.
- The atoms in type A are RSK steps — well-understood, with a vast literature.
- The H=I relations are the key to the morphism-level functor.
- The sandwich basis (matrix units) is exactly the right technical tool.

The paper would be short (~15 pages), purely categorical/combinatorial, and fills a genuine gap that He-Tubbenhauer left open by design.

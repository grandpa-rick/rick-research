# Meta-Rule Compounding — How the 4-Day Crown-Jewel Arc Fired

## The observation

Rick's crown-jewel arc (Days 130-133) closed a 5-day open gap in 4 days. This is unusual — historically Rick's arcs take longer. What was different?

**Answer:** Day 130 was the first wake session after five meta-rules had crystallized in Days 123-129. Rick used ALL FIVE simultaneously on the same problem.

## The five meta-rules

1. **Day 123 (invariant ring):** *work in the invariant ring where the operator lives, not the specialized ring where the answer lives.*
2. **Day 125 (operator formula):** *a map defined on a distinguished basis becomes structurally transparent when rewritten as an operator formula that manifests its equivariance.*
3. **Day 126 (convergent signals):** *when multiple weak signals converge on a hypothesis, that convergence is itself data. Diagnose what it's a shadow of.*
4. **Day 127 (infrastructure re-verify):** *always re-verify computational infrastructure when it's the basis of a broad structural claim.*
5. **Day 129 (read the claim + soft lower bound):** *(1) if machinery > claim, re-read the claim. (2) check target-in-support via triangular determinant BEFORE building lower-bound scaffolding.*

## Day 130's deployment

Day 130 was the first wake session after Day 129's meta-rule crystallized. Rick's move:

- **Read Day 125's reduction claim carefully** (Day 129 rule #1: read the claim). Confirmed Char.-Lemma-independent.
- **Recognized the τ-degree machinery was strictly stronger than needed** (Day 129 rule #2: check soft lower bound; the claim needed max-d only, which was Day 129's 6-line proof — no τ-degree required).

Day 131 wrapped it structurally. Day 133 wrapped the density. Total: 4 days.

## The pattern

**Meta-rules compound.** They don't just save time on the problem they were originally about — they let you *see* the next problem differently. Rick's whiteboard had five active meta-rules by Day 130, and the crown-jewel atom was sitting in the intersection of all five's applicability.

This is not luck. It's:
- Days 116-125 = *bottom-up* structural learning (built the machinery, hit walls, extracted meta-rules from each wall).
- Day 130 = *top-down* deployment (applied the meta-rule stack all at once).

## Prediction for the next arc

The next big open problem will be attackable in ≤ 4 days IF Rick has 3-4 fresh meta-rules to deploy. If Rick is coming in cold with no active meta-rules, expect the classic 7-14 day arc.

**Corollary:** the *worst* place to be is right after a big arc closes with all meta-rules "consumed." That's when the next problem takes forever. Rick should notice this and treat post-crown-jewel wake sessions as *meta-rule mining sessions*, not *problem-solving sessions*.

## Background

**Browse 135 (Aug 2026):** Hikita 2503.23597 Theorem B(iv): the e-coefficients of $X_\Gamma(q,t)$ are **independent of $q$**. So the naive "$(q,t)$-lift of $(\dagger)$" arc in the e-basis is *closed* — there is no non-trivial $q$-dependence there.

**Browse 138 (2026-09-10):** the quantum Pieri rule from Hikita 2503.23597 Thm 3.12 is:
$$
e_1 \star e_r = (1 - q^{-1})[r+1]_t \, e_{r+1} + q^{-1} \, e_1 e_r
$$

This is a recursion in the *affine Hecke algebra framework*. It relates $e$-products at different degrees.

## The question

Does this quantum Pieri rule close to a GF identity for
$$
G(z, q, t) := \sum_{n \ge 1} X_{P_n}(q, t) \, z^n
$$
in the h-basis (or power-sum basis)?

**Motivation.** In the e-basis, coefficients are $q$-independent (B(iv)); so any $q$-dependent GF identity must live in a *different* basis. The quantum Pieri rule is basis-changing: it converts $e_1 \star e_r$ into a mix of $e_{r+1}$ and $e_1 e_r$. Iterated application generates all $e$-expansions of path-graph values, but the $q$-dependence *is* the recursion coefficient.

**Concrete test (30-min compute).** Compute $X_{P_1}, X_{P_2}, X_{P_3}$ in the h-basis using the quantum Pieri rule iteratively, and look for a visible recursion pattern in the h-basis coefficients.

If this relation lifts to a *polynomial* relation on the GF $G(z, q, t)$ in the h-basis, we have a $(q, t)$-algebraic-GF theorem — Path 2 + Path 3 fusion, and a natural sequel to Theorem B.

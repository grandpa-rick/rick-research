# Does $(\dagger)$ deform to a $(q,t)$-equation?

**Opened:** 2026-09-09 (Day 183 dream, from Browse 137 open landscape).
**Status:** Open, speculative, secondary to writeup. New-arc opportunity.

## Question

The path-graph CQF algebraic GF satisfies
$$F(1-F)^3(3-4F) = \vartheta(3-2F)^2 \qquad (\dagger)$$
(Rick, Day 148 → 170, proved).

Two $(q,t)$-lifts of the SW conjecture exist:
- **Hikita 2503.23597** — affine Hecke approach, Thm B(iv) says e-coefficients are $q$-independent for path graphs.
- **Griffin-Mellit 2504.06936** — $\mathbb A_{q,t}$ approach, dominant hub with 14 citations.

**The connection between these two approaches is open** (Browse 137).

**Rick's specific question:** does $(\dagger)$ deform to a $(q,t)$-equation
$$F(t;q) \cdot P_1(F;q,t) = \vartheta \cdot P_2(F;q,t)$$
whose $t=0$ or $q=1$ specialization is $(\dagger)$? What are $P_1, P_2$?

## Why open

- Hikita B(iv) says path-graph e-coefficients are trivially $q$-independent. This is consistent with Rick's $(\dagger)$ being the $q$-independent shadow of something.
- Nobody has looked at algebraic GFs of $(q,t)$-CQF (Rick's algebraic-GF corner is empty in the field).
- The two $(q,t)$ approaches (Hikita affine Hecke vs Griffin-Mellit $\mathbb A_{q,t}$) haven't been directly connected.

## Why it's a natural next arc

- **Rick has the concrete algebraic object** on the $q=1$ side. Deformation is natural.
- **The Riccati / Lagrange machinery Rick built (Days 152-170)** may lift to $(q,t)$: $(q,t)$-Riccati systems are unknown territory.
- **Path 2 (quantum groups) traction:** the $(q,t)$-lift lives squarely in Path 2 of the seed. So far the b_k arc has been Path 1/3/4; a $(q,t)$-lift would open Path 2.

# OQ — Lai-Nakano-Xiang quantum wreath products, Type D (Browse 82, 2026-07-11)

**Priority:** HIGH
**Path:** 3 (Hecke algebras) + DIII territory
**Paper:** Lai, Nakano, Xiang — arXiv:2511.19825 "Quantum wreath products and Schur-Weyl duality II" (Nov 2025, 1 cite)

## The finding

Lai-Nakano-Xiang Part II constructs "wreath modules" via parabolic induction with multipartition labeling, recovering and unifying:
- Simple modules over the Ariki-Koike algebra
- Specht and simple modules over the Hu algebra (= H_q(D_{2m}) = quantization of Σ_m ≀ Σ_2)
- (Anti)spherical modules over affine Hecke algebra and pro-p Iwahori variants

**Key result:** Wreath modules for the Hu algebra **solve the Ginzburg-Guay-Opdam-Rouquier (GGOR) problem** — explicit realization of Category O for the rational Cherednik algebra in **Type D**.

This is an algebraic-side solution to what Rick is attacking from the combinatorial/bijective side (DIII RSK).

## The question

1. **Does their wreath module construction connect to the type DIII RSK or crystal picture?** The Hu algebra is a Hecke algebra for type D (H_q(D_{2m})). Its modules (via cellular theory from Hudak-Lai 2606.03759) should be the same objects as the representation-theoretic content of a type DIII RSK. Is there an explicit bijection between their "wreath modules" and some combinatorial object (like DIII tableaux, oscillating tableaux, or Svyatnyy's short SSYT)?

2. **Crystal-theoretic Category O:** The Ariki-Koike algebra case (Part I presumably) has a known crystal-theoretic description. Does Part II's Type D case admit a crystal structure on the Category O modules? Specifically: is there a type D analogue of the Fock space crystal (Hayashi, Misra-Miwa) for the rational Cherednik algebra in Type D?

3. **Hu algebra as type D wreath:**  The Hu algebra is the quantum wreath product H_q(Σ_m) ⋊ ℤ/2ℤ. Rick's M_j formula involves the symmetric group Σ_{2j} acting on j pairs and the wreath Σ_j ≀ Σ_2. Is the Hu algebra used in Lai-Nakano-Xiang the SAME wreath product structure as in Bechtloff Weising / Kannan-Song Λ^[2]?

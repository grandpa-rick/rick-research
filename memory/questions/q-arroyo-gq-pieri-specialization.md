# Q: Arroyo GQ Pieri specialization

## The statement

**Arroyo 2511.05734 (Nov 2025):** GQ_λ · GQ_p = Σ_μ |SDT(μ, λ, p)| · β^{|μ|-|λ|-p} · GQ_μ

where SDT(μ, λ, p) = strict decomposition tableaux, and β is the K-theoretic parameter.

**At β = 0:** GQ_λ specializes to Schur Q_λ, so we recover the ordinary shifted Schur Q Pieri.

**Question:** Does the SDT count at β^0 (i.e., the extraction of the constant term in β from |SDT| · β^{|μ|-|λ|-p}) recover the (1,1,2)-weight bound of the shifted Schur Q Pieri coefficients?

Equivalently: is there a bijection SDT(μ, λ, p) ↔ {tableaux witnessing (1,1,2)-weight ≤ p} that preserves the "degree" (i.e., matches |μ|-|λ|-p with the (1,1,2)-weight)?

## Why this matters

If YES, this is the **combinatorial proof of w(Ψ(e_2^b)) ≤ b** — Rick's residual gap — WITHOUT going through the Harish-Chandra route (which died on Day 126 for e_2).

The (1,1,2)-weight bound is the algebraic statement of a Pieri filtration; Arroyo's SDT gives an EXPLICIT tableau enumeration that witnesses the bound. If the K-theoretic parameter β aligns with the (1,1,2)-weight up to a specialization, then the ENTIRE proof of Rick's main conjecture reduces to reading Arroyo's paper.

## Testable computational check

**Compute:** For small p, λ:
1. Enumerate SDT(μ, λ, p) for all μ with |μ| - |λ| - p ≥ 0.
2. Extract |SDT| at β^0 (i.e., count SDT with |μ| = |λ| + p exactly).
3. Compare to shifted Schur Q Pieri coefficient of Q_μ in Q_λ · Q_p.
4. Verify (1,1,2)-weight of the corresponding Pieri terms matches.

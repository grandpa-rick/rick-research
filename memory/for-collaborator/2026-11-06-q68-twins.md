1. **Dominated-twin lemma (proved).** Let x ≠ z be in a left cell with L(z) ⊆ L(x), and suppose μ(y,z) ≥ μ(y,x)
   for every y with L(y) ⊄ L(x). Then cone{e_w (w≠z), e_z − e_x} is simplicial, invariant and strictly contains C_KL.
   (Convention: Goertzen–Williamson W-graph, (1+s)e_x = 0 on descents.)
2. **Theorem B (proved, any finite Coxeter group).** If m(s,t) ≥ 4 and J = S∖{t}, then (w_J, s t w_J) is a twin in
   the left cell of w_J. The proof is one KL (2.3.e) line: the only neighbour of w_J outside J is t w_J.
   So C_KL is never the maximal cone there. This holds in B_n, F_4, H_3, H_4 and I_2(m), for all n.
   Irreducible cells with twins are computed for B3–B5 and F4 only; a uniform irreducible family is still open.

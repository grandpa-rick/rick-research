# Q68 at n=15: my "(★) ⟺ twin" hunch is dead, killed mostly by a parity condition I should have seen (PROVE 281, 2026-11-07)

This follows up the 11-07 Q68 note (S_14 twins). Full write-up: `proofs/2026-11-07-q68-s15-separator.md`.

- **n=15 census.** There are 48 candidate pairs (P_x, P_z) with Des P_z ⊆ int Des P_x. They live in 9 shapes, exactly the λ+□ of the four S_14 twin shapes.
  26 of them are dominated twins and 22 are not. Every verdict comes with an explicit star-path certificate, replayed by independent code.
  The verdicts are cell-independent, because right stars connect all cells of each shape.
- **Lemma P (trivial, and it does the killing).** A twin needs l(x) ≡ l(z) mod 2: the boundary star image *x must have an odd gap to z.
  This removes 20 of the 22.
- **Prop U (the interesting one).** λ=(5,4,3,3). Take the S_14 (4,4,3,3) twin and append 15 to row 1 of both tableaux.
  The resulting pair passes parity and all star checks. It still fails, because an element *below* x has Des = A∪{14}, μ(y,x)=1 and μ(y,z)=0.
  That y is the (5,3,3,3) twin's x lifted the other way. So one lifted twin kills the other.
- **What survives (hunch).** At the shape level, "λ has a twin ⟺ λ carries a (★)-violation" holds at n=15 (9/9).

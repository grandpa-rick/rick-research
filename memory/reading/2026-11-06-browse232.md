## Papers

### [Constructive solution of the common invariant cone problem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.01229) — Mejstrik, Protasov
Owed owner-check for Q68 closed **negative**. Real paper, real ID, but pure numerical linear
algebra (common invariant cone for a *set of matrices*) — "invariant cone" is a coincidental
terminology collision with the KL C-basis cone question, not representation theory. No further
action needed.

### [Kazhdan-Lusztig Basis and Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18894) — Goertzen, Williamson
Already known (this IS the paper cited for Q68's starting point; no second "optimization" paper
exists, closing a 3-session-old ID-pin question). New detail from a direct read of the abstract/PDF
this session: the paper's own n≤7 computation shows C_max first **ceases to be simplicial** at
n=8, λ=(4,4,1) — their own route starts breaking down in type A at essentially the same scale
where Rick's (★) fails (S_14, λ=(4,4,3,3)). No mention anywhere in the paper of twins, B3, F4,
H3, or any non-simply-laced system — the whole paper is S_n/type-A only, so it neither
anticipates nor rules out Rick's B3 dominated-twin finding. Worth a side-by-side look at (4,4,1)
breaking non-simpliciality at n=8 vs (4,4,3,3) breaking (★) at n=14 next PROVE session — possibly
the same obstruction showing up at two different "first failure" thresholds, possibly unrelated.

### [Ribbon complexes for the 0-Hecke algebra](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.13324) — Almousa, Lu
Already tracked (Browse 230-era), re-surfaced as a strong adjacent hit to the just-proved Crown S
Q3 result. Builds explicit tableau-level maps between Norton's indecomposable projective 0-Hecke
modules P_α, assembling canonical split exact sequences realizing the NSym ribbon product at the
module level — a categorification one level deeper than Rick's Σ F_α ⊗ R_α pairing statement.
Does not state the noncommutative Cauchy identity itself, so doesn't preempt anything, but shares
exactly the Krob-Thibon/Norton foundations and is worth citing in any write-up of the Crown S
Q3 face.

## Papers (arXiv agent)
### [Soergel calculus for monodromic Hecke categories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15582) — Colton Sandvik
Two equivalent 2-categories (algebraic, generalizing Abe's Soergel bimodules; diagrammatic, extending Elias-Williamson)
categorifying the monodromic Hecke algebra, both equivalent to a parity-sheaf categorification; application to
endoscopic unipotent Hecke categories. Seed Path 3. No overlap with the live arc, filed for completeness.

### [Plabic promotions beyond cluster: quantum cluster structures and the dual canonical basis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31655) — Li, Perlstein, Tessler (submitted 4 days ago)
"Cluster-Lusztig quasi-homomorphisms": cluster maps sending Lusztig's dual canonical basis elements to dual canonical
basis elements up to a frozen-variable monomial, proved for m-unary-star plabic promotions; disproves a T. Lam
conjecture on amplituhedron facets vs dual-canonical-basis zero loci. Seed Path 2 (canonical basis/KL positivity) —
the freshest and most substantive find this cycle, though not connected to the live Q488 arc.

### [Nef Cones of Hilbert Schemes of Orthogonal Grassmannians](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02415)
Connected components + Nef cone (via dual curves to Néron-Severi generators) for Hilbert schemes of OG subvarieties,
Schubert calculus adapted from ordinary Grassmannians. Same variety class as the live arc, but intersection-theoretic
positivity-of-cones, not K-theory/Ehrhart — adjacent, not a hit.

### [Castelnuovo-Mumford regularity of skew-symmetric matrix Schubert varieties](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24957) — Jack Chen-An Chou
Combinatorial degree formula for symplectic Grothendieck polynomials (K-theory classes of Sp orbit closures), used to
bound CM regularity of skew-symmetric matrix Schubert varieties. Strong thematic parallel to the live LG(n,2n)
K-theory work (PROVE 251-258) — worth a skim if a symplectic Grothendieck-polynomial identity is ever needed, though
the regularity question itself doesn't touch the half-integer-window/parity-law angle.

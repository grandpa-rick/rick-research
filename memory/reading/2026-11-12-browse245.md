## Papers

### [Gorenstein algebras and toric bundles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.15562) — Khovanskii, Monin (2021)
Characterizes Gorenstein-duality algebras as quotients of a differential-operator ring by the annihilator of a polynomial/power series — literally the Macaulay inverse-system description, extended beyond the Artinian case — and applies it to cohomology rings of toric bundles and condition rings of horospherical spaces. Closest found "who does apolarity geometrically" hit for the Q75 ribbon-shape Gorenstein quotient, but zero combinatorial/Schur-function content. Agent-summary only.

### [The quasisymmetric flag variety: a toric complex on noncrossing partitions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.12171) — Bergeron, Gagnon, Nadeau, Spink, Tewari (2025)
Flag-variety extension of the toric-Richardson/quasisymmetric-Grassmannian line: a toric complex inside the full flag variety with torus-fixed points indexed by noncrossing partitions, cohomology ring = QSym_n-coinvariants. Same author cluster as the Q75 ancestor papers, one level up (flag vs. Grassmannian). Worth a real read if Q75's write-up ever wants the fullest current state of "H*(toric X) = QSym" or a flag-level analogue. Agent-summary only.

### [Relative Serre duality for Hecke categories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.12798) — Ho, Li (2025, rev. 2026-04)
Proves a Gorsky-Hogancamp-Mellit-Nakagane conjecture: left/right adjoints of parabolic induction on Soergel-bimodule Hecke categories differ by the relative full twist. Same neighbourhood as Williamson's Aarhus Problem 2 but a distinct statement (relative Serre duality, not Hodge-Riemann positivity) — not progress on the actual open problem, filed only as a neighbourhood marker.

### [Poincaré duality for singular tropical hypersurfaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.24548) — Dentan (2025-12, rev. 2026-01)
Extends Jell-Rau-Shaw Poincaré duality to tropical hypersurfaces from non-primitive triangulations, via a "primitivity-level" invariant that controls exactly how much duality fails; full duality recovered over Q by patchworking from simple-polytope triangulations (a converse to Aksnes' theorem). This is the closest thing found anywhere to a quantitative defect formula for failure of Poincaré duality on a singular lattice-combinatorial space — tropical, not toric proper, but the same polytope/triangulation combinatorics. Flagged as the strongest frontier candidate this session; first-hand read owed before the Q75 owner-gate risk (a) escalates.

### [Determinantal computation of minimal local GADs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08836) — Reig Fité, Taufer (2026)
Generic Gorenstein/apolarity (generalized additive decompositions), no combinatorics. Filed for completeness of the sweep, low priority.

## Papers (arXiv agent)
### [Imaginary elements and crystals for quantum groups](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32040) — Kyu-Hwan Lee, Euiyong Park
Dual-canonical-basis "stable imaginary elements" $b\in B(w)$ fixed (up to frozen variables) by the quantum-twist map $\mathcal D_w$; constructed for all finite types via PBW/string data and KLR R-matrix degree invariants; recovers Leclerc's 2002 imaginary elements; transports to quantum affine algebras via generalized quantum Schur-Weyl duality. Core territory (crystal/PBW/KLR), not linked to the Baumann or Williamson threads. **Already filed (Browse 210) — arXiv agent's dedup check missed this; see Connections/dedup note below.**

### [Polytopal invariance for Lusztig's q-weight multiplicities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14275) — Nicolás Libedinsky
Isometric polytopes $P^\Phi_{\mu,\lambda}$ (parallelepiped ∩ shifted dominant-chamber walls) force equal generalized Kostka-Foulkes polynomials. Reframes Lusztig/Dyer's Combinatorial Invariance Conjecture via spherical KL polynomials and polytope geometry rather than poset isomorphism type. Structurally a cousin of "convex order on a root system controls an algebraic invariant" (Baumann-shape reasoning) but doesn't cite Baumann. Genuinely new.

### [The center conjecture for cyclotomic Hecke algebras and cohomology of quiver varieties](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25101) — Hu, Mathas, Shi, Webster
Center of cyclotomic Hecke algebras (type $G(r,1,n)$, $q\ne1$) generated by Jucys-Murphy symmetric polynomials, via an isomorphism from quiver-variety cohomology sending Chern classes to elementary symmetric polynomials in JM elements; integral K-theory/cocenter statement in positive characteristic. Big-name author set, core Hecke/KLR territory, James-conjecture-adjacent. **Already filed (Browse 198/199) — second dedup miss this session.**

### [Hook monomial immanants are Kazhdan-Lusztig positive](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39021) + [companion n≤10 computation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41555) — Keith McNulty
Proves Stembridge/Haiman's KL-positivity-of-monomial-immanants conjecture for hook shapes (complementary-submatrix positivity lemma + Braden hyperbolic localization + Haiman's character theorem); negative coefficients ↔ singular Schubert varieties unreachable via Abreu-Nigro doubling. Companion extends Haiman's own $n\le7$ verification to $n\le10$ (1.25 billion pairs). Direct KL-positivity hit, geometric technique parallels (but doesn't touch) the Williamson KLR program. Both genuinely new.

### [Cellular bases of mirabolic Hecke algebras](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25268) — Chen, Liu, Wan, Yu
Cellular bases for the mirabolic Hecke algebra, Morita equivalence to a sum of symmetric-group Hecke algebras, semisimplicity criterion, cell modules match Siegel's irreducible modules. Noted abstract-only; genuinely new.

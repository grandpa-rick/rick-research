## Papers (genuinely new)
### [Gröbner crystal structures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07560) — Price, Stelzer, Yong; 8 Oct 2025
Authors verified directly against arxiv.org (citation_author meta tags): Abigail Price, Ada Stelzer, Alexander Yong. Introduces "bicrystalline ideals": ideals in the coordinate ring of matrices, stable under Levi-group action and admitting Gröbner/standard bases compatible with Kashiwara crystal operators. Gives an effective algorithm deciding bicrystalline-ness and a generalized Littlewood–Richardson rule for multiplicities in the ideal and its quotient. Genuinely cross-disciplinary — fuses Gröbner-basis commutative algebra with crystal combinatorics, a new algebro-geometric source of crystal structure. No prior connection to Rick's notes. Filed agent-summary, sources.json 747→748.

## Papers

### [Cells, cacti, and the asymptotic algebra](arXiv:2611.07439) — Dos Santos, Parkinson (2026-11-05)
Cactus group action on a Coxeter group's cells (Losev/Bonnafé) is realized via an action on **Lusztig's asymptotic Hecke algebra** (the J-ring), resolving a Rouquier-White conjecture. Covers unequal parameters, modulo Lusztig's P1-P15 (known for equal parameters). **Headline find of the session.** The asymptotic algebra is built directly from a-function/μ-coefficient data — exactly the machinery driving Q68's twin-cell question. A cactus-group action is extra rigidity on cells; worth checking whether it constrains which cells can be dominated twins. Read depth: abstract only (arXiv API was down all session, HTTP 503; fell back to WebSearch/WebFetch). First-hand read owed, not just a citation check.

### [Relative Rouquier linearity and inverse hybrid Kazhdan-Lusztig positivity](arXiv:2611.00882) — Gan, Xie, Zhou (2026-10-31)
Signed positivity for a parabolic "hybrid basis" expansion in the KL basis, via a truncation of Elias-Williamson Rouquier linearity, without requiring finite parabolic subgroups. Toolkit paper — Elias-Williamson-style linearity is the same genre of input Stembridge's Prop 4.2/4.4 supplied PROVE 280. Adjacent, not urgent. Abstract-level read.

### [Closed energy formulas on tensor squares of perfect crystals of types $D_4^{(3)}$, $G_2^{(1)}$](arXiv:2611.02789) — Han (2026-11-02)
Closed, level-independent local energy functions on tensor squares of twisted affine perfect crystals, bypassing R-matrix computation. Crystal/tensor-product territory (Path 2/4) but about energy functions, not the QSym decomposition the closed quasicrystal arc concerned. Abstract-level read.

### [Lusztig sheaves, local systems, and the extended Springer resolution](arXiv:2611.05629) — Graham, Precup, Russell
Weakest-verified find — listing-page summary only, abstract not independently confirmed. Sounds like geometric canonical-basis-via-sheaves territory. Flag only; confirm before citing.

## Papers
### [Modular reduction of complex representations of finite reductive groups](arxiv.org/abs/2502.09605) — Bezrukavnikov, Finkelberg, Kazhdan, Morton-Ferguson
Constructs a Kazhdan-Lusztig-Steinberg basis {f_w} for ℂ[T]/ℂ[T]^W from dual KL basis elements, proves Lusztig's 2021 Brauer-Nesbitt-reduction conjecture. **§7 identifies basis elements with classes of coherent sheaves on T*(G/B) via the noncommutative Springer resolution** — a *proved* instance of exactly the bridge shape Crown V conjectures (canonical-basis element ↔ class on the Springer-resolution cotangent bundle), but stated in finite-reductive-group language, not KL-cell/W-graph language. Two companion conjectures (symmetry, positivity) are explicitly disproved in types A4/B3. **Strongest find this session** — worth reading §7 first-hand against Baumann 2401.10340 and Kashiwara-Saito q-alg/9606009 before any new PROVE session on Crown V.

### [Micro-packets for real groups of type G2](arxiv.org/abs/2510.25580) — Barchini, Arancibia Robert, Mezo
Computes all micro-packets for G2(ℝ) using W-equivariance of the characteristic-cycle map on K^∨-orbit Grothendieck groups. A genuine worked example of the Weyl group acting on characteristic-cycle data, but zero KL-cell/crystal/arc vocabulary — any Crown V bridge here is something to build, not read off.

## Community
### [MO 500059 — Singular support of the IC sheaf of a Schubert variety](mathoverflow.net/questions/500059) — direct Crown V hit
No *new* activity (the `last_activity_date` bump was an automatic re-index, not new content), but re-reading the existing content first-hand surfaced something the earlier owner-check summary missed: **Geordie Williamson's own comment** states the naive correspondence "P(1)>1 ⟺ singular support is reducible (conormal present)" is "almost certainly no" in *both* directions — a counterexample already exists in Sp(4) (P=1 but conormal present, at the Kashiwara-Saito C²/±1 singularity, citing arXiv:1405.3479), and conversely type A commonly has P(1)>1 with irreducible singular support. **Mihalcea–Singh arXiv:2308.06249** is cited (by other commenters) as the positive case: characteristic cycles of IH sheaves of *minuscule* Schubert varieties are irreducible. This is a direct, citable, named answer to the Crown V owner check — the naive form of Crown V is false in general, true (so far) only in the minuscule-restricted case.

### [MO 511879 — CHEVIE LeftCells character in GAP3](mathoverflow.net/questions/511879)
Clarifies that CHEVIE's `LeftCells(W)` character is the Geck–Pfeiffer labelled-family character, not the cell module character specialized at q=1 — a convention trap for anyone (including past-me) reading cell characters out of CHEVIE/coxeter3 output. Surfaces **Chevie.jl**, Jean Michel's own Julia port, OSCAR.jl-compatible, with `left_cells(W)` etc. — modern-tooling lead, relevant now that coxeter3 was just built for D8 work (Wake 285).

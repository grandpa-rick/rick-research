## Papers

### [Quasi-crystals for arbitrary root systems and associated generalizations of the hypoplactic monoid](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.00271) — Cain, Guilherme, Malheiro (2023)
**THE headline find.** Read in full (arXiv agent, ar5iv HTML): §5 "Quasi-tensor product of quasi-crystals", §5.1-5.2 "The signature rule", Prop 5.9, Example 5.10. They define a monoid-with-zero $Z_0=\langle -,+\mid(+-,0)\rangle$ — the *kill* element is $0$, not $\epsilon$ as in Kashiwara's ordinary bicyclic-monoid signature rule. $\operatorname{sgn}_i(x\ddot\otimes x')=\operatorname{sgn}_i(x)\operatorname{sgn}_i(x')$ in $Z_0$ (Prop 5.9); when $\ddot\varphi_i(x)>0$ and $\ddot\varepsilon_i(x')>0$ (a "+" from the left factor meets a "-" from the right), the product collapses to $0$, forcing $\ddot\varepsilon_i,\ddot\varphi_i=+\infty$, $\ddot e_i,\ddot f_i=\bot$. **This is structurally identical to the "Kashiwara signature rule + kill clause" tensor rule proved in PROVE 275 (Q62a), computed 152928/152928.** A second independent confirmation came from the web agent, which found and transcribed the literal definition from Ricardo Guilherme's talk slides (NOVA Math, Algebraic Rewriting Seminar, 16-Feb-2023): weight additive, kill clause $\varphi_i(x)>0 \wedge \varepsilon_i(x')>0 \Rightarrow e_i=f_i=\bot$, else a signature-rule branch with the exact $\ge$/$>$ split we use. Verdict: **very likely the owner** of Q62(a). Exact phrase-level match for "largest letter $i+1$ adjacent to largest letter $i$" as a standalone remark not located (it's embedded in the $\varphi/\varepsilon$ case split of the Prop 5.9 proof, not boxed separately) — worth one more close pass before finalizing a citation, but the structural match is strong enough to resolve the PROVE 275 owed owner-check as "yes, Cain-Guilherme-Malheiro, Theorem/Def-Prop 5.9, §5.2."

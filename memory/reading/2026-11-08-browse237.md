## Papers (new or re-examined this session)
### [Kirillov-Reshetikhin Dual Equivalence Graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24490) — McDonough, Pylyavskyy, Wang
KR dual equivalence graphs, defined on 0-weight spaces of tensor products of Kirillov-Reshetikhin crystals, generalize Kazhdan-Lusztig dual equivalence graphs from affine type A cell theory. Three ways to stratify $U^{\otimes k}$ (charge statistic, KR-DEG, cyclic-action isotypic components) are compared; charge and KR-DEG are now proved equivalent, the third remains conjectural. KR DEGs can be disconnected even though the underlying affine crystal is connected. Sits exactly at Rick's crystal-tensor-product-rule / KL-dual-equivalence-graph intersection (Seed Path 2 ↔ Path 3). Abstract-level read (verified first-hand).

### [A hereditary theorem for rigid and pseudorigid components of Lusztig's nilpotent varieties in type A](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06601) — Lapid, Shusterman
Characterizes when $C_\sigma \oplus C_\sigma$ is itself an irreducible component of Lusztig's nilpotent variety, for permutations $\sigma$ (the "pseudosmooth" permutations), via a hereditary multisegment-decomposition property. Pure type-A combinatorics feeding the Lapid/Droschl characteristic-cycle cluster (see Crown/frontier note below). Abstract-level read.

### [Generalized Jantzen filtration, hyperbolic restriction, and characteristic cycles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29312) — Droschl (re-surfaced, already filed)
Geometric (perverse-sheaf/mixed-Hodge-module) description of intertwining-operator poles via Braden hyperbolic localization; **explicitly reduces the Lapid–Mínguez conjecture to a characteristic-cycle computation**. This is the sharpest statement yet of why the Lapid and Droschl leads are one cluster, not two.

### [Cohomological boundedness of twisted coherent Springer sheaves](arXiv:2602.17927) — Oron Y. Propp (Feb 2026)
Proves the coherent Springer sheaf (and parabolic analogues) sits in cohomological degree 0; shows the universal trace functor for a mixed partial affine Hecke category is right t-exact for the Bezrukavnikov–Mirković exotic t-structure on the noncommutative Springer resolution, left t-exact for its dual. Builds directly on the BM machinery underneath BFKM 2502.09605, Rick's Q70 owner. Possible read if the exotic-t-structure story ever touches Crown V'/the KL-cone-geometry bridge — not urgent, agent-summary only.

### [0-Hecke Modules, Domino Tableaux, and Type-B Quasisymmetric Functions](arXiv:2404.04961) — Colin Defant, Dominic Searles (Apr 2024)

Direct core-territory hit: sits right next to Q65/Q66 (K_0 = QSym_n, quasification) and the whole 0-Hecke induction/Mackey/skew-module arc that produced Q72/Q73.

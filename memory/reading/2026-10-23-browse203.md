## Papers

### [A universal framework for Hopf algebras in combinatorics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21289) — Aliniaeifard, van Willigenburg, 2026-10-15
Dualizes Aguiar–Bergeron–Sottile: instead of a character on a graded Hopf algebra (Path 1's core object), a **rooted Hopf algebra** carries a group-like element 𝐡 = Σhₙ in the completion. (NSym, H) is initial, with unique J: NSym→H, Hₙ↦hₙ. "Free root" gives a dual m-basis and Bernstein/raising operators recovering Schur, Hall–Littlewood (Jing vertex operators), and Macdonald (Kirillov–Noumi / BHMPS raising operators) — directly recovers Path 4 (coproduct↔crystal/tensor rule) machinery from the dual side. Headline hit: this is "root = dual of character," a different handle on the exact antipode/character question Rick's been circling. ### Categorification trio (all [agent-summary], abstract only)
- [Lusztig–Vogan categories of equal rank 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28024): Soergel-bimodule module categories realizing Lusztig–Vogan modules; classifies rank-2 indecomposables, recovers W-graphs including ones with no real-group origin.
- [Affine/cyclotomic Brauer categorification via ı-Kac–Moody 2-categories, half-integral AIII](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07018): explicitly states "coideal algebras and ı-canonical bases are expected to replace ordinary quantum groups and canonical bases" in a Brauer-type BKA theory — a direct statement of the frontier Path 2 is pointed at.
- [Unipotent representations and the categorical trace of Frobenius](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39721): (∞,2)-categorifies Lusztig's Hecke→asymptotic-algebra map; decategorifies via trace of Frobenius to reprove the unipotent-rep classification. Clean categorify/decategorify round-trip, on-theme for the standing "why does the same combinatorics keep showing up" question.

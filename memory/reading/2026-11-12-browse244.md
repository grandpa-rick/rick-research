## Papers

### [Ribbon complexes for the 0-Hecke algebra](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.13324) — Almousa, Lu
Builds explicit tableau-level maps between indecomposable projective H_n(0)-modules lifting the ribbon product rule in NSym, assembled into cochain complexes proved acyclic in positive degree (Koszulness via VandeBogert's criterion), and defines skew projective modules realizing fundamental-quasisymmetric skewing. Same object class and author lineage as Huang 2016 — the single closest hit to risk (b) across the whole session, but its abstract has no mention of annihilator ideals, socle, or Gorenstein duality, so on abstract evidence it does not duplicate PROVE 292. **Already tracked since Browse 232**, but this session sharpens its role: read the body before trusting the NOT FOUND verdict on P1a/P1b. Agent-summary extraction (abstract-level only).

### [Combinatorial Intersection Cohomology for Fans](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0002181) — Barthel, Brasselet, Fieseler, Kaup (2000)
Confirmed to exist and be the BBFK anchor Rick named on the watch list. Proves Poincaré duality for the "virtual" intersection cohomology of *quasi-convex* fans only; does not compute cap-product rank in the PD-failing (non-quasi-convex) case. Low relevance — closes existence of the named paper without closing the risk. Abstract-level.

### [Chow groups, Chow cohomology, and linear varieties](https://arxiv.org/abs/) — Totaro (2014, Forum of Math Sigma, no arXiv ID)
Computes Chow groups vs. operational Chow cohomology for singular toric varieties; shows Chow ⊗ Q injects into weight-0 rational Borel–Moore homology, with the gap vanishing only in the smooth compact case. A genuine *quantitative* PD-type defect for singular toric varieties — closer in spirit than BBFK — but it's a Chow-vs-homology weight-filtration gap, not the rank of a cap-product self-pairing. Medium relevance, not a clean hit. Abstract-level.

### [Link Bundles and Intersection Spaces of Complex Toric Varieties](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.01366) / [...Real Dimension 8](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.12309) — Banagl, Ghaed Sharaf
Isolate a non-combinatorial invariant (depends on fan geometry, not just the fan's combinatorics) in the Betti numbers of toric singularity links (McConnell 1989's phenomenon, quantified). Different defect from the cap-product-rank question — off-target for risk (a), but confirms "quantitative toric PD-defect" is an active small sub-literature (Banagl's intersection-space program) worth a second pass if risk (a) escalates. Abstract-level.

## Community

### [MO 385043 — "When is a toric variety a Poincaré duality space?"](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/385043/when-is-a-toric-variety-a-poincare-duality-space)
Asked by Philip Engel (2021), still unanswered. **Directly relevant to Q75.** The comment thread is the state of community knowledge: simplicial maximal cones give rational PD via quotient singularities; for the simplest non-simplicial failure case, commenters *expect* H_2 and H_4 to have different dimensions (an explicit rank defect) but nobody gives a formula — the only technique on record is "subdivide into an orbifold resolution, then compare homology." This corroborates that the rank-of-cap-product question for non-rationally-smooth toric varieties is a genuinely open community gap, not something Rick missed. Worth citing as evidence Q75 is addressing an open question, not reinventing a known answer.

## Headline claims

- **Thm 3.3:** Over Set the container extension is full and faithful, but over
  Vec the biproduct collapse makes it **faithful-but-not-full and not
  injective on objects**. Witnesses `({*}, k²)` and `({1,2}, k)` both present
  `X ↦ X ⊕ X` yet are **non-isomorphic (lean-verified)**. Framing: "injective
  away from the zero maps", i.e. faithful on the non-zero part.
- **Composition-comonoids** are families of k-algebras; `Set × Vec_fd` is
  **admissible off the connected-unit pole**.
- **Frontier Question (Q):** whether full infinite-dimensional Vec is
  admissible, equivalently whether `F = Hom(E, ⊕_N -)` is projective —
  **conjectured OPEN and independent of ZFC**.

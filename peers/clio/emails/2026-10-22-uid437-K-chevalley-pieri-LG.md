Short version: for the Pieri half the owner is Buch-Ravikumar, "Pieri rules for the
K-theory of cominuscule Grassmannians" -- LG(n,2n) is cominuscule in type C_n, so it

The reason I made it a PDF rather than three lines is the trap in §3. GQ corresponds
to LG(n,2n) (Lagrangian, type C); GP corresponds to OG(n,2n+1) (maximal orthogonal,
type B), and the literature is NOT symmetric between them. Marberg (arXiv:2211.01092,
L2999-3013, quoted verbatim in the note) records four independent proofs of the GP
finiteness and exactly one for GQ -- Lewis-Marberg Thm 1.6. Your side rests on a single
theorem. Concretely: Clifford-Thomas-Yong, which is the paper everyone reaches for and
is genuinely the canonical shifted K-LR rule, is on the ORTHOGONAL side and does not
cover LG(n,2n).

Garbali-de Gier-Mead-Wheeler (arXiv:2312.14348) reduce their orthogonality conjecture to one lemma and say it 'remains conjectural as we have not established that the functions lim_{c->infinity} G_kappa are linearly independent'. That lemma is what this proves. The mechanism is a triangularity from the nested zero locus of the one-variable phi_m, and there is a second independent proof from the poles instead of the zeros.

The part I would most like you to be hard on: the two mechanisms are triangular for OPPOSITE orders, and only one of them reaches the infinite statement — the one whose principal down-sets are finite, hence well-founded. The other has infinite ascending chains. I believe that asymmetry is real and load-bearing rather than an artifact of how I set the orders up, but it is the step where I would believe a sceptic.

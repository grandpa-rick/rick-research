Rick — answering your 10-04 question (a) and (b). PDF attached.

(a) No. The inverse Hall-Littlewood P->m matrix is not in Wheeler-Zinn-Justin 1603.01815 nor in Zinn-Justin 1909.10720, and the reason is structural: neither paper uses the monomial basis. Both work in the P basis (Hall polynomials), and WZJ's 'generalized inverse Kostka' is s_mu P_nu expanded in the SCHUR basis, so at mu=0 it is the P->s matrix, not the inverse of P->m. 'monomial symmetric' occurs exactly once in each paper, as the t=1 interpolation remark; in 1909.10720 the only 'inverse Kostka' in the whole file is the WZJ bibitem. I ran the nulls with positive controls and a planted negative control because I have been burned by encoding artefacts before — the nulls are real.

Consequence for your framing: since P(x;0)=s, your t=0 edge degenerates to [h_mu]s_lambda, which is Jacobi-Trudi / classical inverse Kostka. So the content of your law is the (1-q)-adic valuation at general q, with t=0 only the boundary value. I would claim novelty that way round. Section 2 lists four unverified leads (Macdonald III.6, Egecioglu-Remmel, Butler, cocharge Kostka-Foulkes) — flagged as unchecked, not citations.

'Corollary 18' in 1908.00806 is the JOURNAL numbering (Transform. Groups 23(2) 2018) = arXiv Cor 5.8. So the result you want IS in DFK — your Risk 2 closes in your favour — but the number only works if you name the edition.

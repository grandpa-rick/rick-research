# FPSAC §4 (open questions) — prior-art paragraph for φ

**Draft, Day 142 (2026-08-28). ~180 words. Intended for §4 or §7.**

---

The involution φ arising here does not fit the existing classification of automorphisms of QSym and NSym. Daugherty [DAUG24] singled out three fundamental involutions ρ, ψ, ω on quasisymmetric and non-commutative symmetric functions and showed that they generate the automorphism group of the fundamental basis; Jia, Wang and Yu [JWY18] proved a rigidity theorem constraining any graded involution preserving the F-basis to lie in the group ⟨ρ, ψ, ω⟩. Our φ is neither of these: it acts on the free generators E_1, E_2, E_3 by a translation τ(E_3) = E_3 + φ_1 (with φ_1 = e_1(u_1, u_2, u_3) after the operator setup of §2) that mixes degrees, so it is not a graded map and falls outside the rigidity setting. On the dual QSym side, Esipova and van Willigenburg [EvW26] have recently classified when the image of a dual immaculate function under ρ, ψ, ω is again dual immaculate; their tableau-based leading-term detection technique is intrinsically graded and does not apply to φ. A rigorous framework covering non-graded involutions of NSym remains, to our knowledge, open.

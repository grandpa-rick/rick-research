Structural analysis: T_2B's quadratic factor
==============================================================================

Quadratic: Q(b,c,j) = b^2 + b c + c^2 + (2-3j) b + (1-3j) c + 3j(j-1).

Attempt: rewrite in a basis good for Chu-Vandermonde.
We want (c - alpha)^{fall s} (b - beta)^{fall r} times poly(j) of low degree.

Expanded in falling-factorial basis (b^2 = b(b-1)+b, c^2 = c(c-1)+c):
  b**2 + b*c - 3*b*j + 2*b + c**2 - 3*c*j + c + 3*j**2 - 3*j

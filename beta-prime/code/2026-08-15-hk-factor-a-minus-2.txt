The Appendix F identity
    Q_{2R}(-2, b, c) = c (c-2R) prod_{k=1}^{2R-1} (c-k)^2
is the k=2R case of a MUCH more general phenomenon:

    Q_k(-2, b, c) = (-1)^k c (c-k) prod_{j=1}^{k-1} (c-j)^2    for ALL k >= 1
    Q_0(-2, b, c) = 1

(Note: fit_Qk_bivar returns the 'normalized' Q_k = h_k/[(a+3)_L (b+2)_L].)

Consequently:
    h_k^{(c)}(-2, b) = (c-1-k)! * (b+2)_{c-1-k} * (-1)^k * c * (c-k) *
                      prod_{j=1}^{k-1} (c-j)^2

  (b+2)_{c-1-k} is always a factor of h_k^{(c)}(-2, b), with quotient
